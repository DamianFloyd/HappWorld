Mission Statement

HappyWorld provides inspiration to people by educating them about the major issues affecting the world, its life forms and providing solutions and initiative to solve them.

Core Values

Sustainability - Solutions that protect the world for future generations. Environmental Stewardship - Maintaining the ecosystems and act to preserve them. Education & Awareness - Providing knowledge to inspire change and to inform. Respect for Nature - Showing respect for the world and all aspects of it.
