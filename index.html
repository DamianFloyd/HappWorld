Our Story

Welcome to our home page! We’re excited to share our journey, vision, and values with you. HappyWorld website was created based on the lack of knowledge and concern that individuals have about the major issues facing the world. It seeks to impart both reliable and accessible information that is easy to understand, with the hope of inspiring people to make a positive difference. The website will provide educational games to increase user awareness and will further give real world tips to foster an ecofriendly community. The website was inspired by the SDG’s (Sustainable Development Goals) developed in 2015 which aims to address world challenges such as climate change and life below water.
