The third Annual HappyWorld Marathon

The third annual marathon will be held at Koga Park on June 15th, 2025. The course this year is approximately 5.5.km across the beautiful terrane of the park, the breath-taking views of the city and along the boardwalk overlooking the ocean. Runners will be given a special numbered T-shirts along with a first aid kit from one of our sponsors. Volunteers are also welcome along the route to handout water and other refreshments to the runners. Participation in the marathon is free, however as always, your donations are welcome.

HappyWorld Tree Planting Day 2025

Saving the trees around the world is the priority of all its inhabitants. The HappyWorld family is pleased to be hosting this event at the Omiya Wildlife Reserve for the third year. Each volunteer will be planting at least two trees on the day using the gardening equipment provided. The event will be streaming live on the global wildlife network. Trees help to absorb (CO2) and release clean oxygen to help fight climate change and cool the planet. These trees will provide homes for birds, insects and other lifeforms from your efforts.

Annual HappyWorld Eco Concert 2025

The HappyWorld Eco concert is aimed at building awareness about the issues affecting the environments around the world, like climate change. It will bring together like-minded people from different backgrounds for a mixture of entertainment, socialization and educational messages to ignite interest in environmental activism. This fund-raising event will be donating its proceeds directly to the research facility for climate change at the Phoenix University of Science. There will be Eco-friendly vendor boots and green tech companies giving short presentations on clean energy..
